---
quote: |-
  You have the right to work only but never to its fruits.
  Let not the fruits of action be your motive, nor let your attachment be to inaction.
author: Bhagavad Gita, 2.47
source: https://www.holy-bhagavad-gita.org/chapter/2/verse/47/
encountered: 9 Jul 2026
commentary: |-
  I came across this when reading [this article](https://www.notboring.co/p/the-company-as-a-machine-for-doing). This quote so concisely portrays how I felt when I was working on ClaimKit. Millions of dollars potential. Possibly zero. If it may be worth nothing, was doing the work worth it? The answer I consistently came to was no, not this work, not on this subject, and not in this way.

  This idea is perhaps a better-phrased cousin of "love the process." You must do what you enjoy — otherwise, if the fruits never arrive, you have wasted your time. And the fruits may never arrive, and should not be expected to. The process is your life, not the outcome at the end.
order: 7
